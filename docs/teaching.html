I teach statistics for psychology students and have supported a range of courses in psychology and cognitive science as a teaching assistant.

As instructor

Statistics for the Behavioral Sciences
PSYCH-UA.10 · Department of Psychology, New York University
An introduction to statistics for psychology majors: evaluating data from different kinds of studies, statistical prediction, and applying statistical reasoning to decision making. Students learn to analyze data in R and write up results in APA format, working through data description, variability, significance tests, confidence intervals, tests of differences of means, correlation, linear regression, and nonparametric methods.
